# Feature 2: "The Rebound" (Post-Interview Autopsy)

## Epic 2.1: Interview Data Ingestion
- **Story 1: Voice-to-Text Debrief.** Instant capture of thoughts after the interview.
- **Story 2: Transcript Parsing.** Support for Zoom/Meet .vtt files.
- **Story 3: Culture Logger.** Capturing 'vibe' and friendliness of interviewer.
- **Story 4: Challenge Library.** Extracting the single hardest question faced.
- **Story 5: Lifecycle Tagging.** Tracking drop-off at Screening, Tech, or HR rounds.

## Epic 2.2: Technical Accuracy Analysis
- **Story 1: Semantic Correctness.** AI fact-checks user's technical answers.
- **Story 2: The Perfect Response.** Generates the ideal code/answer for failed points.
- **Story 3: False Confidence Zones.** Flags wrong answers given confidently.
- **Story 4: Depth of Why.** Scores if user explained principles or just syntax.
- **Story 5: Remedial Links.** Map failures to specific MDN/YouTube documentation.

## Epic 2.3: Behavioral & Soft Skill Critique
- **Story 1: Filler Word Detection.** Tracking 'um', 'ah', and 'like'.
- **Story 2: Answer Length.** Flagging rambling vs. too-short answers.
- **Story 3: STAR Validation.** Checks for Result-oriented storytelling.
- **Story 4: Question Quality.** Evaluates the questions the candidate asked back.
- **Story 5: Tone Heatmap.** Sentiment analysis of emotional arc (Desperate vs. Calm).

## Epic 2.4: Strategic Recovery Actions
- **Story 1: Clarification Email.** AI-drafted follow-up correcting a technical slip.
- **Story 2: Follow-up Cadence.** Reminder system for pings (24h, 7d).
- **Story 3: Negotiation Scripts.** Preparation for success scenarios.
- **Story 4: Post-Failure Code Patch.** Suggests a GitHub fix for the failed live coding task.
- **Story 5: Resilience Prompting.** CBT-based reframing of rejection.

## Epic 2.5: Aggregate Growth Analytics
- **Story 1: Trend Analysis.** Visualizing improvement across last 5 interviews.
- **Story 2: Rejection Categorization.** Identifying if issue is Skill, Culture, or Pay.
- **Story 3: Success Correlation.** Finding which topics lead to 2nd rounds.
- **Story 4: Readiness Forecast.** Predicting when user will likely get an offer.
- **Story 5: Career Journey Export.** A growth report for second-chance employers.
