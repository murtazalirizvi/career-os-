# Feature 4: "Persona-Play" (Adversarial Mock Interviews)

## Epic 4.1: Multi-Persona Archetype Engine
- **Story 1: Stone-Faced Architect.** Zero positive reinforcement simulation.
- **Story 2: Rushed Founder.** High-speed, interruptions-based interview.
- **Story 3: Non-Tech HR.** Metaphor and 'ELI5' focused questions.
- **Story 4: Deep-Diver.** Layered 'Why' questions on one project.
- **Story 5: Blind Mode.** Randomized persona selection.

## Epic 4.2: Real-Time Sentiment Analysis
- **Story 1: Filler Word Tracker.** Real-time detection of 'Um/Ah'.
- **Story 2: Pitch Analysis.** Detecting 'Upspeak' and lack of confidence.
- **Story 3: Silence Detection.** Differentiating thinking from freezing.
- **Story 4: Tone Correlation.** Categorizing energy levels.
- **Story 5: Gaze Tracking.** Visual analysis of eye contact.

## Epic 4.3: Deep Logic Questioning
- **Story 1: Trade-off Trigger.** Forcing downsides analysis of every choice.
- **Story 2: Scale-Up Scenario.** Sudden constraint changes mid-interview.
- **Story 3: Collaborative Debugging.** AI and user fixing code together.
- **Story 4: Behavioral Traps.** Testing professionalism under negative prompts.
- **Story 5: Negotiation Curveball.** Sudden salary talk in tech rounds.

## Epic 4.4: Dynamic Feedback & Coach
- **Story 1: The Whisperer.** Real-time hints if stuck.
- **Story 2: Transcript Breakdown.** Line-by-line persona critique.
- **Story 3: Improvement Heatmap.** Comparison with previous sessions.
- **Story 4: Alternative Universe.** AI showing the 'Senior' version of your answer.
- **Story 5: Pass/Fail Badges.** Persona-specific certifications.

## Epic 4.5: Voice & Video Synthesis
- **Story 1: Low-Latency Audio.** High-fidelity AI voices.
- **Story 2: Lip-Sync Avatar.** Talking head for immersion.
- **Story 3: Env Simulation.** Zoom/Office backgrounds.
- **Story 4: Multi-Language.** Urdu-English (Hinglish) support.
- **Story 5: Share-for-Review.** Generating links for human mentors.
