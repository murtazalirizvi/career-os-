Feature 01 · Resume Intelligence

Lens Engine

Multi-dimensional resume analysis: visual layout scoring, ATS compatibility, semantic job-match, and benchmark comparison against top candidates — all powered by Gemini AI.

🚨 The Problem

Most engineers submit resumes blind — no idea if the ATS will parse it, whether the layout hurts readability, or how it stacks up against other applicants for the same role.

Outputs You Get

ATS Score Visual Layout Score Semantic Match % Benchmark Rank Heatmap Gemini Coaching

How to Use It

  • 1 Navigate to Lens Engine in the sidebar
  • 2 Upload your resume as a PDF file
  • 3 Paste the job description you're targeting (optional but recommended)
  • 4 Click Analyze — AI processes layout, ATS tags, and semantic alignment
  • 5 Review your score breakdown and heatmap highlighting weak zones
  • 6 Read Gemini coaching recommendations and apply targeted fixes

🧠
Feature 02 · Post-Interview Intelligence

Interview Rebound

Paste your interview notes or upload an audio transcript. AI generates a multi-dimensional autopsy: technical depth, behavioral signals, strategic positioning — plus a week-long drill plan targeting your weakest area.

🚨 The Problem

After a tough interview, most engineers either spiral into self-doubt or move on without learning anything. Neither helps. You need a structured debrief that turns the experience into a concrete improvement plan.

Outputs You Get

Technical Score Behavioral Score Strategic Recovery Score Practice Drills 7-Day Plan Audio Transcription

How to Use It

  • 1 Go to Interview Rebound right after your interview
  • 2 Paste your interview notes or transcript into the text area — or upload an audio file for AssemblyAI transcription
  • 3 Add context: role, company, interview type (technical / behavioral / system design)
  • 4 Click Run Autopsy — AI scores each dimension and identifies your weakest area
  • 5 Review the drill exercises generated specifically for your gaps
  • 6 Follow the 7-day improvement plan before your next round

Feature 03 · Market Intelligence

Skill Arbitrage

Enter your target role and region. AI fetches live job market data, identifies your skill gaps, builds a 90-day sprint plan, and calculates the ROI of learning each skill — with salary data in USD, PKR, and GBP.

🚨 The Problem

The tech skill landscape shifts constantly. Engineers waste months learning skills that are already commoditized or irrelevant in their target market. You need data-driven prioritization, not gut feel.

Outputs You Get

Skill Gap Map 90-Day Sprint Plan ROI per Skill USD / PKR / GBP Salaries Market Demand Score Learning Resources

How to Use It

  • 1 Open Skill Arbitrage and enter your target role (e.g., "Senior Backend Engineer")
  • 2 Select your region — affects salary benchmarks and market demand weighting
  • 3 List your current skills so the AI can calculate your actual gaps
  • 4 Click Analyze Market — AI fetches live job data and runs gap analysis
  • 5 Review the ROI-ranked skill list — highest salary impact per learning hour at the top
  • 6 Download your 90-day sprint plan with weekly milestones and resource links

Feature 04 · Mock Interview Simulation

Persona Coach

Choose an interviewer persona and go live. Real-time coaching hints appear as you speak. Filler word tracking, gaze analysis, and post-session reports with a personalized study guide. Supports Hinglish.

🚨 The Problem

Practicing with friends gives you softball questions. LeetCode doesn't simulate the social pressure of a real interview. You need adversarial, realistic practice that exposes your actual weak spots under pressure.

Outputs You Get

Real-Time Hints Filler Word Count Gaze Analysis Coaching Report Study Guide Hinglish Support
Available Personas

⚡

📋
Non-Tech HR — Behavioral focus, culture fit, soft skills
🔭
Deep Diver — Follows every thread, tests depth of knowledge

How to Use It

  • 1 Open Persona Coach and select an interviewer persona
  • 2 Set the interview type: technical, behavioral, system design, or mixed
  • 3 Enable camera and microphone for gaze tracking and speech analysis
  • 4 Start the session — the AI persona asks questions in character
  • 5 Watch real-time coaching hints appear in the sidebar as you answer
  • 6 After the session, review your coaching report and generated study guide

Feature 05 · Story Generation

Narrative Architect

Paste a GitHub repo URL. AI analyzes your code architecture, generates STAR-format interview stories, creates a LinkedIn post, builds a portfolio site, and cross-checks consistency with your resume.

🚨 The Problem

Engineers build great things but struggle to articulate them. "I built a microservice" doesn't land. You need structured STAR stories that connect your technical decisions to business outcomes — and most people don't know how to write them.

Outputs You Get

STAR Stories LinkedIn Post Portfolio Site Architecture Analysis Resume Consistency Check Tech Stack Summary

How to Use It

  • 1 Open Narrative Architect and paste your GitHub repo URL
  • 2 Optionally upload your resume PDF for consistency cross-checking
  • 3 Click Analyze Repo — AI reads your code structure, README, and commit patterns
  • 4 Review the STAR stories generated for each major technical decision
  • 5 Copy the LinkedIn post draft and the portfolio site HTML
  • 6 Check the consistency report — fix any gaps between your code and resume claims
