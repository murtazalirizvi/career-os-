## 🎯 Quick Reference Guide

### "Where can I see the practice questions?"
**Answer:** Read **PRACTICE_QUESTIONS_LOCATION.md**

**TL;DR:** Practice questions appear in the **"Autopsy Results"** panel (top-right) after you:
1. Run Full Autopsy
2. Click "Generate Practice Questions"

They have **purple styling** and appear **below** the autopsy scores.

---

### "What are recovery actions doing?"
**Answer:** Read **RECOVERY_ACTIONS_EXPLAINED.md**

**TL;DR:** Recovery actions are your **post-interview playbook** that includes:
1. **Follow-up email** - Send to interviewer within 24h
2. **Study plan** - Fix your weakest areas in 7 days
3. **Negotiation guide** - Maximize compensation if you get an offer
4. **Resilience prompt** - Stay motivated after rejection

They appear in the **"Recovery Actions"** panel (bottom-right).

---

### "What do all the buttons do?"
**Answer:** Read **REBOUND_BUTTONS_EXPLAINED.md**

**TL;DR:**
- **Run Full Autopsy** - Complete AI analysis (30-60 sec)
- **Quick Debrief** - Fast 30-second analysis
- **Load Trend** - Track progress over multiple interviews
- **Generate Practice Questions** - 3 AI questions targeting your weakest area

---

### "How do I use The Rebound?"
**Answer:** Read **REBOUND_COMPLETE_USER_GUIDE.md**

**TL;DR Workflow:**
1. Fill in interview details (company, role, round, vibe, notes)
2. Click "Run Full Autopsy" (wait 30-60 sec)
3. Review scores in "Autopsy Results" panel
4. Click "Generate Practice Questions" (wait 5-10 sec)
5. Review practice questions (purple styling, below scores)
6. Review recovery actions in "Recovery Actions" panel
7. Send follow-up email within 24h
8. Complete study plan within 1 week
9. After 2+ interviews, click "Load Trend" to track progress
