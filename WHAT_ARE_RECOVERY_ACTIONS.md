1. **Follow up** with the interviewer
2. **Fix weaknesses** before your next interview
3. **Negotiate** if you get an offer
4. **Stay motivated** after rejection

## 🎯 What Recovery Actions Include

### **1. Follow-Up Email Template** 📧

**What it is:**
A professionally written email to send to your interviewer after the interview.

**When to use:**
- Within 24 hours of the interview
- Whether you think you passed or failed
- Shows professionalism and interest

**Example:**
```
Subject: Thank you for the opportunity - [Your Name]

Dear [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me about the Backend 
Engineer role at Google. I enjoyed learning about your team's work 
on distributed systems and the challenges you're solving.

Our discussion about database scaling reinforced my interest in 
the position. I've been reflecting on the sharding question we 
discussed, and I wanted to share some additional thoughts on 
trade-offs between consistency and availability...

I'm excited about the possibility of contributing to your team 
and would welcome the opportunity to continue our conversation.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Why it works:**
- Shows you're thoughtful and engaged
- Demonstrates you learned from the conversation
- Keeps you top-of-mind
- Can turn a "maybe" into a "yes"

---

### **2. Clarification Email** 🤔

**What it is:**
An email to ask for feedback or clarify next steps.

**When to use:**
- If you're unsure about your performance
- If the interviewer mentioned something unclear
- If you want to address a mistake you made

**Example:**
```
Subject: Follow-up question - Backend Engineer role

Hi [Interviewer Name],

Thank you again for the interview yesterday. I wanted to follow up 
on the database scaling question we discussed.

I realized after our conversation that I could have explained the 
trade-offs more clearly. Specifically, when choosing between sharding 
and replication, the key factors are:

1. Read vs write patterns
2. Data consistency requirements
3. Operational complexity

I'd love to hear your thoughts on this approach if you have a moment.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Why it works:**
- Shows you're self-aware
- Demonstrates you're learning
- Gives you a second chance to impress

---

### **3. Negotiation Scripts** 💰

**What it is:**
Pre-written scripts for salary negotiation conversations.

**When to use:**
- After receiving an offer
- During the offer discussion call
- When negotiating benefits

**Example:**
```
SCENARIO: They offer $110k, you want $130k

YOU: "Thank you so much for the offer! I'm really excited about 
the opportunity to join the team. I was hoping we could discuss 
the compensation package.

Based on my research and the market rate for Backend Engineers 
with my experience in [your city], I was expecting a range closer 
to $125-135k. Is there flexibility in the base salary?"

THEM: "We can go up to $120k."

YOU: "I appreciate that! Could we also discuss equity and signing 
bonus to bridge the gap? I'm looking at a total compensation of 
around $135k when factoring in equity."

THEM: "Let me check with the team and get back to you."

YOU: "Perfect! I'm excited to work together on finding a package 
that works for both of us."
```

**Why it works:**
- Anchors at a higher number
- Shows you've done research
- Leaves room for compromise
- Maintains positive tone

---

### **4. Resilience Prompt** 💪

**What it is:**
A motivational message to help you bounce back from rejection.

**When to use:**
- After getting rejected
- When feeling discouraged
- Before your next interview

**Example:**
```
🌟 Reframe: This interview produced diagnostic data, not a personal verdict.

Your next leverage points are:
- Technical depth (46.0) → Study distributed systems patterns
- STAR precision (25.0) → Write 3 quantified stories
- Reduce filler words (8 detected) → Practice with timer

Convert one weak answer into a documented practice drill within 24 hours.

Remember: Every "no" is data. Every interview makes you sharper. 
The offer is coming — you're building the skills to earn it.
```

**Why it works:**
- Reframes rejection as learning
- Gives specific action items
- Keeps you motivated
- Focuses on improvement, not failure

---

### **5. Strategic Actions List** 📋

**What it is:**
A prioritized list of things to do before your next interview.

**When to use:**
- Immediately after the interview
- During your preparation for the next one
- To track your improvement

**Example:**
```
Strategic Actions (Priority Order):

1. URGENT (Next 24 hours):
   ☐ Send follow-up email
   ☐ Study distributed database patterns
   ☐ Write 1 STAR story about a system design project

2. HIGH (Next 3 days):
   ☐ Practice explaining sharding vs replication
   ☐ Reduce filler words (record yourself)
   ☐ Review CAP theorem and consistency models

3. MEDIUM (Next week):
   ☐ Build a mini-project with Redis caching
   ☐ Practice 5 system design questions
   ☐ Update resume with quantified metrics
```

**Why it works:**
- Gives clear next steps
- Prioritizes what matters most
- Tracks your progress
- Builds momentum

   - Time-sensitive actions (follow-up email) at top
   - Long-term improvements (study plan) below

## 🎯 Why Recovery Actions Matter

### **Without Recovery Actions:**
- ❌ You don't know what to do next
- ❌ You repeat the same mistakes
- ❌ You feel discouraged after rejection
- ❌ You miss opportunities to follow up

### **With Recovery Actions:**
- ✅ Clear action plan for improvement
- ✅ Professional follow-up emails ready
- ✅ Negotiation scripts prepared
- ✅ Motivation to keep going
- ✅ Track your progress over time

## 💡 Pro Tips

### **Tip 1: Act Fast**
- Send follow-up email within 24 hours
- Start study plan immediately
- Don't wait until your next interview

### **Tip 2: Customize Templates**
- Don't copy-paste emails verbatim
- Add personal details from your interview
- Make it sound like you, not AI

### **Tip 3: Track Completion**
- Check off actions as you complete them
- Review before your next interview
- See how you've improved

### **Tip 4: Combine with Practice Questions**
- Use Recovery Actions for strategy
- Use Practice Questions for drilling
- Together they make you interview-ready
