# 🚀 Quick Start - The Rebound

## ⚡ 5-Minute Guide to Your First Interview Autopsy

---

## Step 1: Access The Rebound (30 seconds)

1. Open your browser: **http://localhost:5500**
2. Sign in (or use demo mode)
3. Click **"The Rebound"** in the left sidebar (🎤 icon)

---

## Step 2: Fill in Interview Details (2 minutes)

In the **top-left panel** ("Interview Details"), fill in:

```
Company: [e.g., "Acme Corp"]
Role: [e.g., "Frontend Engineer"]
Interview Round: [Select: Technical / Behavioral / Final]
Interviewer Vibe: [Select: Friendly / Neutral / Tough]

Voice Notes / Debrief:
[Type or paste your notes, e.g.:]
"I struggled with the system design question about caching.
The interviewer asked about distributed systems and I wasn't
confident in my answer. I did well on the React questions though."

OR

Transcript / VTT:
[Paste your interview transcript if you have one]
```

---

## Step 3: Run Full Autopsy (1 minute)

1. Click **"Run Full Autopsy"** button (top-right header)
2. Wait 30-60 seconds for AI analysis
3. Watch the status bar for progress

---

## Step 4: Review Your Results (1 minute)

Look at the **top-right panel** ("Autopsy Results"):

```
✅ Overall Score: 64.14
✅ Technical: 44.41 ⚠ WEAK!
✅ Behavioral: 73.09 ✅ STRONG
✅ Recovery: 85 ✅ STRONG

⚠ False confidence zones: system design, caching
Filler words: 8 | STAR compliance: n/a
```

**What this means:**
- Your **technical skills** need work (44.41/100)
- Your **communication** is strong (73.09/100)
- You're **good at follow-up** (85/100)
- You thought you knew system design but didn't (false confidence)

---

## Step 5: Generate Practice Questions (30 seconds)

1. Click **"Generate Practice Questions"** button (top-right header)
2. Wait 5-10 seconds
3. Scroll down in "Autopsy Results" panel to see questions

**You'll see:**
```
🎯 Practice Questions
Focus area: technical (score: 44.4/100)

Q1: Design a distributed caching system that handles 1M requests/sec.
    How would you ensure consistency across cache nodes?

Q2: How would you handle race conditions in a multi-threaded web server?
    What synchronization primitives would you use?

Q3: Explain the trade-offs between SQL and NoSQL databases for a
    social media feed. When would you choose each?
```

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Use Quick Debrief for Phone Screens
If you just had a quick 15-minute phone screen:
1. Add your notes
2. Click **"Quick Debrief"** instead of "Run Full Autopsy"
3. Get instant feedback in 10-20 seconds

### Tip 2: Practice Questions Out Loud
Don't just read the practice questions:
1. Answer them out loud
2. Record yourself
3. Time yourself (2-3 minutes per question)
4. Compare to your original interview

### Tip 3: Track Your Progress
After 2-3 interviews:
1. Click **"Load Trend"** button
2. See your improvement over time
3. Celebrate wins, focus on weak areas

### Tip 4: Customize the Follow-Up Email
Don't copy-paste verbatim:
1. Add specific details from your interview
2. Mention the interviewer's name
3. Reference a topic you discussed
4. Include a question or additional insight

## ⚠️ Common Issues

### "I don't see practice questions!"
**Solution:** 
1. Run "Full Autopsy" first (required)
2. Click "Generate Practice Questions"
3. Scroll down in "Autopsy Results" panel (top-right)
4. Look for purple styling

### "Button is grayed out"
**Solution:**
- Fill in ALL required fields (company, role, round, vibe, notes)
- Run "Full Autopsy" before "Generate Practice Questions"

### "Autopsy failed"
**Solution:**
1. Check backend is running: http://localhost:8000
2. Check Gemini API key in Backend/.env
3. Try again (API may be temporarily unavailable)
