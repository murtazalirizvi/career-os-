## ❓ PART 5: ANTICIPATE & ANSWER QUESTIONS

### Q: "Why should Job Seeker X use this instead of LinkedIn's built-in features?"

**Your answer:**
> "LinkedIn is a network. Career OS is a coach. LinkedIn shows you job openings. Career OS shows you **why you're getting rejected** and **what to do about it.** It's not competitive — you use both. LinkedIn finds the job. Career OS makes sure you win it."

### Q: "How do you differentiate from Interviewing.io or other mock interview platforms?"

**Your answer:**
> "Those are single-feature tools. Career OS is a **system.** Your mock interview failure feeds into your study plan. Your study plan feeds into your next resume update. Your resume update feeds back into your readiness score. It's a flywheel. That's defensible. That's hard to copy."

### Q: "What's your business model?"

**Your answer:**
> "Freemium. Free tier: Basic resume analysis, one mock interview. Premium: Unlimited features, priority feedback, integration with LinkedIn/GitHub. We're targeting individual job seekers eventually, but right now we're exploring partnerships with universities and retraining programs. **High volume, low churn.**"

### Q: "Who are your users?"

**Your answer:**
> "Career-switchers and long-term unemployed. People over 30 re-entering the market. Recent bootcamp grads. **Anyone who can't afford to fail.** They're desperate for a system. We give them one."

### Q: "How do you handle the AI hallucination risk?" (Security / Safety)

**Your answer:**
> "Excellent question. Three guards: One, we don't generate code, we critique it. Two, all feedback is checked against documentation (MDN, GitHub Docs). Three, users can contest any feedback — it goes to our human review queue. **We're not automating judgment. We're automating pattern recognition.** Safer."

### Q: "What happens when the market changes?"

**Your answer:**
> "Our data pipeline scrapes job boards weekly. Feature 3 (Skill Arbitrage) recalculates every 7 days. The system adapts. That's a key assumption — **if the market shifts, our recommendations shift with it.** No stale data."

## 💬 PART 6: THE VIBE — HOW TO DELIVER

### Tone Guidelines

**DO:**
- Speak like you're explaining to a smart friend, not a tech conference
- Use concrete examples ("6-second resume scan" vs abstract "ATS optimization")
- Own the gaps ("We're not done with Feature X yet")
- Show pride in the system, not desperation

**DON'T:**
- Over-explain the tech (they don't care about the Gemini API)
- Use jargon without translating it
- Apologize for what you didn't build
- Speak faster as you get nervous (judges notice)

### Body Language

- **Posture:** Stand tall. Hands out of pockets. Movement = energy.
- **Eyes:** Look at the judges, not the screen. Reference the screen, but address them.
- **Pacing:** Move slowly. Let silence exist. Silence is confidence.
- **Hands:** Use them to point, to gesture. Don't fidget.
