# Career OS Demo Script (Member 5)

## 3-Minute Main Script

### 0:00 - 0:20 Opening
"Career OS turns job hunting from guessing into intelligence. Instead of fragmented tools, we give candidates one system that shows what is working, what is missing, and what to do next."

### 0:20 - 1:00 Feature Walkthrough (Quick Pass)
1. "Feature 1 checks resume readiness for ATS and recruiter visibility."
2. "Feature 2 turns interview outcomes into concrete recovery actions."
3. "Feature 3 maps your skills to live market demand and identifies high-ROI gaps."
4. "Feature 4 simulates interview personas so practice feels real, not theoretical."
5. "Feature 5 converts project work into clear interview narratives."

### 1:00 - 2:35 Dashboard Final Reveal
"Now this is the unifying layer: the Career OS dashboard."

"This live readiness score combines three real signal groups:"
- "Resume signals from Feature 1"
- "Interview signals from Feature 2"
- "Market-fit signals from Feature 3"

"The breakdown matters because candidates usually optimize one area and ignore the rest. Career OS keeps those signals aligned."

"Even with partial data, the dashboard still works. We normalize the available signals, so users get directional guidance from day one and better precision as more activity is captured."

"The next-action panel converts insight into action, so this is not just reporting, it is execution guidance."

### 2:35 - 3:00 Close
"Career OS is a system, not a single feature. We turn uncertainty into measurable progress, and that is why this product is hackathon-ready and scalable."
